# One Word From Your Ex

One word — the word my ex would use to describe me — entered in the [One Word From Your Ex](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2) bounty on make.fun.

## The word

> Unread

The word an ex reaches for after weeks of messages left on read: short, specific, and painfully familiar to anyone who has been ghosted — or done the ghosting.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python standard library only) copies `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, falling back to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entrypoint, a small stdlib-only copier.
- `entry.txt` — the one-word entry (6 characters).
- `LICENSE` — MIT.
